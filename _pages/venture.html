Alongside my research I work across early-stage venture capital: running the venture community at Oxford, investing into deep-tech and AI-native companies, and building quantitative tools for sourcing and diligence.

President, Oxford Venture Capital Network

I am President of the Oxford Venture Capital Network (OxVCN), Oxford's venture capital society. The Network connects students with founders and investors through speaker events, educational VC workshops and its flagship Venture Capital Programme, which trains aspiring analysts.

I lead the events programme, using my own and the society's network to bring investors and operators to Oxford, and running workshops and hackathons in partnership with other university societies. Past and upcoming events are on the Events page.

Investor, Proximo Ventures

I am an investor at Proximo Ventures, an early-stage fund backing deep-tech founders out of the top UK universities (Oxford, Cambridge and Imperial) across climate tech, life sciences and Industry 4.0. The model puts investors on the ground at the universities they cover: sourcing companies, running due diligence and working directly with founders.

I am also an angel investor in Assuric, one of our portfolio companies.

Venture Scout, Ellipsis Ventures

I scout for Ellipsis, a Zurich-based fund writing first cheques into AI-native companies at pre-seed and seed. Ellipsis is led by Yariv Adan, co-founder of Google Assistant and Google Lens across seventeen years at Google, alongside founding partners including Matthias Dantone, and runs its sourcing, diligence and operations on an autonomous agentic system.

The mandate is companies where AI is the product rather than a feature on top of one, with defensibility coming from proprietary data and deep domain expertise.

Research Intern, Vela Partners

I was a research intern at Vela Partners, a San Francisco fund that describes itself as the first product-led, AI-powered venture firm, and which publishes much of its AI-for-VC research with the University of Oxford.

My work there applied quant-finance backtesting discipline (point-in-time data, leakage controls, survivorship-free universes, pre-registration) to venture signals: predicting which research ideas compound rather than fade, and which founder profiles produce outliers. Vela now runs an extended version of the first of these in house as a monthly research-trends product. The write-ups are on the Quantitative VC Research page.

🎓 I am a DPhil student in Medical Science at the University of Oxford, based at the [MRC WIMM Centre for Computational Biology](https://www.imm.ox.ac.uk/research/units-and-centres/mrc-wimm-centre-for-computational-biology), having previously worked as an ML Computational Biologist at [Nucleome Therapeutics](https://nucleome.com/) and completed my Master's in Bioinformatics and Theoretical Systems Biology at Imperial College London.

🧬 My primary research interests are in the applications of machine learning to multi-omics anlaysis and gene expression regulation. 

🏢 Having developed a research portfolio across my BSc in Biochemistry, my MSc in Bioinformatics and a year in industry, I now apply that biological foundation and computational skillset to AI architectures for 3D genetics. 

💼 Alongside my research I work in early-stage venture capital. I am President of the [Oxford Venture Capital Network](https://oxfordvcnetwork.com/), an investor at [Proximo Ventures](https://proximo-ventures.com/) and an angel investor in [Assuric](https://www.assuric.com/), and a venture scout for [Ellipsis](https://ellipsis-venture.com/), which backs AI-native companies at pre-seed and seed. I was previously a research intern at [Vela Partners](https://vela.partners/), where my work applied quant-finance backtesting discipline to venture signals. More on the [Venture Capital](/venture-capital/) page.

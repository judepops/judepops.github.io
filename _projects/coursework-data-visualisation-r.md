Coursework 2 for the third-year Bioinformatics module: an extended, fully reproducible
analysis written in R Markdown.

The dataset records 11 morphological and motility measurements for 6 animals (3 treated,
3 control) across several time points, each measured with and without injury. The
question: does treatment alter cell shape or cell movement in the context of injury?

The write-up covers the full analysis path in `tidyverse` and `ggplot2`: reshaping the
data, visualising it to expose the treatment/injury structure, and testing the
differences statistically.

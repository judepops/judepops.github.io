An experimental design exercise built around the construction and use of the expression
vector **pFXN-AcGFP1-N1**, encoding human frataxin (isoform 1 preproprotein) with a
C-terminal GFP fusion.

The proforma required:

- Designing the gene-specific portions of forward and reverse primers to amplify the
  frataxin coding region, keeping the reading frame intact for the C-terminal fusion.
- Working from the cDNA insert in pOTB7 (IMAGE:4842134), translated with EMBOSS Sixpack
  to resolve the three forward open reading frames.
- Planning the downstream use of the construct once expressed.

A short structural biology essay on **KcsA**, the prokaryotic potassium channel from
*Streptomyces lividans* and the first K+ channel to be characterised by X-ray
crystallography (MacKinnon, 1998).

The essay covers:

- The physiological role of K+ conduction in cell volume regulation, hormone secretion
  and electrical impulse formation.
- The conserved **TVGYG** signature sequence in the selectivity filter, shared across
  prokaryotes, eukaryotes and archaea.
- How the channel achieves >10,000-fold selectivity for K+ over the smaller Na+ ion while
  conducting at close to the diffusion limit, which is the structural basis of the paradox.

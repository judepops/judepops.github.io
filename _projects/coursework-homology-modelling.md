Coursework 2 for Principles of Bioinformatics, supervised by Prof. Franca Fraternali.
Each student was assigned a different UniProtKB/TrEMBL protein with no solved structure.

The assignment was a guided essay taking the sequence through a full comparative
modelling workflow:

- Template identification and sequence–template alignment.
- Building the homology model and assessing its quality.
- Bioinformatic investigation of the modelled protein's likely function, using the
  structural model as evidence alongside sequence-level annotation.

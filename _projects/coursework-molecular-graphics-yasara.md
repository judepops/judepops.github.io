Practical worksheet for the third-year Protein Structure and Design module: a computer
modelling practical using **YASARA** (Krieger, Koraimann & Vriend, *Proteins* 47, 393–402).

The practical uses the package to build, edit, model and simulate protein structures,
working through molecular graphics techniques for studying protein architecture and
relating it to function.

Coursework 1 for the third-year Bioinformatics module, written up in R Markdown.

The UNIX section works through the command-line toolkit a bioinformatician actually
uses day to day: navigating the directory tree, inspecting and filtering files, and
chaining commands together, with a worked example for each command rather than a bare
definition.

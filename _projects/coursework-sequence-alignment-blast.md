Coursework 1 for the second-year Principles of Bioinformatics module, covering the
mechanics of sequence alignment rather than its application.

Topics worked through:

- **BLAST scoring**: cumulative match/mismatch scores, gap opening and gap extension
  penalties, and computing the total score for a given alignment.
- **Global versus local alignment**: when end-to-end alignment is appropriate (sequences
  of comparable length and similarity) versus aligning conserved sub-regions only.
- Interpreting alignment output and the effect of substitution matrices on the result.

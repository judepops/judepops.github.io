An in-person panel bringing together founders and venture operators to examine investor
relations and what actually secures venture backing.

**Panellists**

- Lily Elsner and Nick Shipley, Jack Fertility
- Anse Asif, [Proximo Ventures](https://proximo-ventures.com/)

**Format:** 45 minutes in total. 3 minutes of introductions, 32 minutes of moderated discussion,
and 10 minutes of audience Q&A.

**Themes covered**

- *The decision to raise.* How founders know it is the right moment to go out for equity, the
  tension between continuing to build and stopping to fundraise, and the signals that tell an
  investor a founder is raising at the right time rather than too early.
- *Non-dilutive funding as a launchpad.* How grant funding got the team into a position to raise
  a proper round, how competitive those applications were and what had to be presented, whether
  grants change how investors perceive a company, and the long-term constraints they can impose
  on IP and operations.

# Genetics coursework figures

Figures from molecular biology courseworks at King's College London (2022): an experimental
design for a frataxin-GFP expression vector, and a lab report on cloning and expressing
His-tagged tissue plasminogen activator.

For the coursework pages in `_projects/`.

| Image | What it shows | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `pfxn-acgfp1-n1-plasmid-map.jpg` (hero) | The designed vector pFXN-AcGFP1-N1 (5,341 bp) in SnapGene: frataxin fused to AcGFP1 under the CMV promoter, cloned between XhoI and PstI | Experimental design proforma, Fig 3 |
| `fxn-gfp-start-junction.jpg` | The start of the fusion: the forward primer's clamp and XhoI site at the frataxin start codon | Proforma, Fig 4 |
| `fxn-gfp-end-junction.png` | The frataxin to AcGFP1 junction, with GFP in frame | Proforma, Fig 5 |
| `paskplat-plasmid-map.png` | The pASKPLAT expression construct (4,293 bp) in SnapGene: the PLAT insert with an N-terminal His tag under the tet promoter | Lab report, Fig 6 |
| `restriction-digest-gel.jpg` | Agarose gel checking pOTB7PLAT: ladder, uncut plasmid, EcoRI, PstI, and EcoRI with PstI | Lab report, Fig 2 |

The plasmid maps are designs made in SnapGene, not verified constructs. Crops and sizes are
recorded in `_cowork/scripts/prepare_undergrad_images.py`; captions, numbers and context are in
`_cowork/notes/undergrad/05_cw3_genetics.txt` (local only).

# Bioinformatics coursework figures

Figures from bioinformatics courseworks at King's College London (2022): homology modelling of a
human chymotrypsin-like protease, differential expression with DESeq2, and visualisation and
statistics in R.

For the coursework pages in `_projects/`.

| Image | What it shows | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `homology-models-superimposed.png` (hero) | Homology models of CTRL-1 superimposed in PyMOL against AlphaFold2, with the loops that differ and the secondary structure that agrees | Homology modelling coursework, Fig 8 |
| `alphafold-model-and-pae.jpg` | The AlphaFold2 model of P40313 and its predicted aligned error | Same coursework, Fig 7 |
| `catalytic-triad-active-site.jpg` | Close-ups of the two template models: a loop, a helix and the His, Asp and Ser catalytic triad | Same coursework, Fig 9 |
| `ramachandran-plots.jpg` | Ramachandran plots (MolProbity) for the two homology models and AlphaFold2 | Same coursework, Fig 12 |
| `deseq2-ma-plot.png` | MA plot for a HOXA1 knockdown against control, with significant genes highlighted | Sequence analysis coursework, Fig 5 |
| `deseq2-sample-distances.jpg` | Sample-to-sample distances: knockdown and control cluster apart | Same coursework, Fig 2 |
| `r-correlation-matrix.png` | Pearson correlations between 11 cell shape and movement measurements (corrplot) | R coursework, p19 |
| `r-cell-measurements.png` | The 11 measurements per animal in cells without injury (ggplot2) | R coursework, p3 |

Crops and sizes are recorded in `_cowork/scripts/prepare_undergrad_images.py`; captions,
numbers and context are in `_cowork/notes/undergrad/03_cw1_bioinformatics.txt` (local only).

# Protein design and docking coursework figures

Figures from computational protein design courseworks at King's College London (2022): a
molecular modelling practical in YASARA, a report on human serum albumin, and a report on the
KcsA potassium channel.

For the coursework pages in `_projects/`.

| Image | What it shows | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `dhfr-docking-tmp-vs-pyr.jpg` (hero) | Trimethoprim and pyrimethamine docked into a simplified DHFR active site, with hydrogen bonds, electrostatic contacts and ring stacking marked | Modelling practical, Q26 |
| `dhfr-docking-surfaces.jpg` | The same two drugs docked alike, with distances, and surface views showing why pyrimethamine cannot bind the same way | Modelling practical, Q25 |
| `dhfr-methotrexate-structure.jpg` | Dihydrofolate reductase bound to methotrexate (PDB 3DFR), with its eight-stranded beta sheet highlighted | Modelling practical, Q16 |
| `hsa-domains-and-electrostatics.jpg` | Human serum albumin in PyMOL: the six subdomains, surface electrostatics from APBS, and the Y and T shaped domain junctions | Albumin report, Fig 1 |
| `hsa-gallic-acid-docking.jpg` | Gallic acid docked into subdomain IIA in YASARA, and the residues around it | Albumin report, Fig 4 |
| `hsa-fatty-acid-binding-sites.jpg` | The 17 disulphide bonds and free Cys34, and fatty acids in the seven fatty acid sites including drug sites Sudlow I and II | Albumin report, Fig 2 |
| `kcsa-channel-structure.jpg` | KcsA in the membrane with pore dimensions, and with one subunit hidden to show the pore helices and the helix bundle crossing | KcsA report, Fig 1 |
| `kcsa-selectivity-filter.jpg` | The selectivity filter: four potassium binding sites formed by backbone carbonyl oxygens and the Thr75 hydroxyl | KcsA report, Fig 2 |

Crops and sizes are recorded in `_cowork/scripts/prepare_undergrad_images.py`; captions,
numbers and context are in `_cowork/notes/undergrad/04_cw2_protein_design_and_docking.txt`
(local only).
